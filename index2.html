02 — Case study

BinWatch — smart waste,
monitored live.

My Final Year Project at Universiti Sultan Zainal Abidin (UniSZA): IoT sensors, a Firebase backend and a Flutter app with three role-based portals for hostels and campuses.

Problem

Hostel bins overflow before cleaners are alerted. Supervisors have no visibility into bin status, and reports are tracked on paper — leading to missed pickups and accountability gaps.

Solution

Ultrasonic IoT sensors report fill levels in real time to Firebase. Supervisors assign tasks, and cleaners receive instant full-screen push alerts in the Flutter app.

Impact

Fewer overflow incidents, automated approval workflows, and live data and reports for evidence-based collection schedules and cleaner deployment.

02 System architecture

Sensors stream fill levels to Firebase Realtime Database. Firestore stores user roles, tasks and reports. The Flutter app surfaces three role-based portals, each subscribed to live data.

04 How it works, end to end

  1. 01

    Sensor reads fill level

    An ESP32 + ultrasonic sensor measures fill % every few seconds and publishes it to Firebase Realtime Database.

  2. 02

    App classifies status

    The Flutter app categorises each bin as Low / Medium / High / Full using 30 / 60 / 90% thresholds.

  3. 03

    Supervisor assigns task

    When a bin hits High or Full, the supervisor assigns a collection task to the nearest cleaner with a priority.

  4. 04

    Cleaner is alerted

    A high-priority full-screen Android notification wakes the cleaner's device — even when idle.

  5. 05

    Task completed & logged

    The cleaner empties the bin and marks it done; the sensor confirms the new level and Firestore logs the event.

  6. 06

    Admin reviews analytics

    Live KPIs and history — full-bin frequency, cleaner performance, hostel hot-spots — guide resourcing.
